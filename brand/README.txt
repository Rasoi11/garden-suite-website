LOGO USE — quick rules
────────────────────────────────────────

  DO
  • Use Primary on Paper, Cream, or Sage backgrounds
  • Use Reverse on Ink or Forest backgrounds
  • Maintain clear space = height of the sprig mark
  • Switch to Mark only below 24mm print / 120px screen

  DON'T
  • Place on saturated or photographic backgrounds without a wash
  • Stretch, distort, or rotate
  • Recolour the leaves
  • Add drop shadows, glows, or strokes
  • Bilingualise the wordmark
