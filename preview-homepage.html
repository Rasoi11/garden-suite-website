Weddings at Garden Suite

Welcome to Garden Suite

Garden Suite is a flexible, family-run venue with beautiful garden views. A setting that suits every kind of celebration.

From Intimate to Grand

Picture yourself saying "I do" surrounded by manicured gardens. Dancing the night away under sparkling chandeliers. Raising a toast with countryside views stretching as far as the eye can see. With a collection of beautiful spaces to choose from, you'll find the venue that feels right for you. Take a look and see which one speaks to your heart.

Traditional Weddings

Celebrate your love with timeless elegance at Garden Suite. Our traditional weddings offer classic charm, stunning ceremony spaces and a picture-perfect backdrop.

Asian Weddings

As one of Swansea's top Asian wedding venues, we have a wealth of experience and stunning event suites that transform into the most beautiful of spaces.

Birthday Celebrations

Whether you're planning a birthday celebration, an anniversary, a family gathering or a professional conference, Garden Suite offers the perfect setting to make every occasion special.

A Serene Countryside Location

Set in the tranquil surroundings of Pontlliw, Swansea, Garden Suite combines countryside charm with effortless accessibility. Just off M4 junctions 47 and 48, with complimentary on-site parking and only 15 minutes from Swansea city centre.

A flexible setting for both intimate gatherings and larger celebrations.
